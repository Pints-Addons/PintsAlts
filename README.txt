Pint's Alts v1.3 - every character on your account, at a glance
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone. Log into each character once so it can record itself.
  Optional: Pint's Craft Upgrades (docks in as a tab), Pints Recipe Sources (trainer-recipe data),
  Pint's Auctions or Auctionator (auction prices in the recipe book).

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsAlts
  Restart the game.

HOW TO USE
  /alts (or /ledger, /pl), or the minimap button (left-click: Ledger, right-click: Craft Upgrades).
  Search box (top right): find any item across every character's bags, bank, mail and gear.

LEDGER TAB - one row per character, grouped by realm, sortable, with totals
  Roster          level (with XP), rested XP, gear score, money, played time, last online
  Bag Space       bags and bank: used / free slots, when the bank was last checked
  Skills          professions, Cooking, First Aid, Fishing - click a profession to open that
                  character's recipe book (see below)
  Activity        mail waiting, when the mailbox was checked, auctions, bids
  Miscellaneous   guild, hearthstone location, class / spec, last zone
  Hover a character for level, zone, XP, professions. Shift + right-click removes one.

RECIPE BOOK (click a profession on the Skills view)
  Every recipe that character knows, grouped like the profession window, in skill-up colours
  (orange / yellow / green / grey, as of the last time they opened it). Search box and a
  filter by slot or type (Head, Hands, Feet, Legs, Weapons, Bags, Trade Goods, Consumables...;
  enchants file under their slot). Auction price for each crafted item. Click a recipe for its
  cost to make: each reagent priced (vendor prices marked "(Vendor)", "not on AH" when unseen),
  raw materials for reagents you can craft yourself (bolts, bars...), the cheapest total and
  the profit if sold. Shift-click a recipe to link it in chat.
  Each character must open each profession window once for its book to be saved.

CHARACTERS TAB
  Character   equipped gear, stats and resistances, item level and gear score. Click any slot
              to try on other gear and see the stat changes: your bags and bank, your alts'
              bags and bank, what your characters can craft, dungeon and raid drops, world
              drops and quest rewards. The character you're on shows as a turnable 3D model;
              others show a race portrait.
  Gear Grid   every character's gear side by side
  Bags        any character's bags and bank
  Reputation  each character's reputations
  Skills      each character's skills
  Hover any gear slot for craftable upgrades from your characters' recipes (with Pint's Craft Upgrades).

CRAFT UPGRADES TAB (needs Pint's Craft Upgrades)
  Your characters down the left; craftable gear that's an upgrade for the one you pick, or
  "Craft for Alts": what the picked character can make that would upgrade the others.
