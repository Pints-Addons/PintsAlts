Pint's Alts - Changelog

v1.4.2
- WoW Forever: money shows with the gold, silver and copper icons (the game's own coin text showed bare digits).

v1.4.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.
- Forever characters saved under a first name alone are cleared once; each saves itself again at its next login.

v1.4
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Professions and skills are read from the modern profession list.
- Recipe books refresh when a profession window opens; game events a client doesn't have are skipped instead of erroring.

v1.3
- First public release.
- Ledger tab: Roster, Bag Space, Skills, Activity and Miscellaneous views for every character, with totals.
- Recipe books: click a profession on the Skills view to browse that character's recipes - skill-up colours,
  slot / type filter, search, auction prices, cost to make with vendor prices and raw materials, chat links.
- Characters tab: gear, stats, gear grid, bags, reputation, skills; try-on with stat changes; 3D model for the
  character you're on, race portraits for the others.
- Craft Upgrades tab (with the Craft Upgrades add-on): pick any character, or Craft for Alts.
- Item search across every character's bags, bank, mail and gear.
